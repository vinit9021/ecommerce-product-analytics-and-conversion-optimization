# Business Problem

## Context

The Google Merchandise Store receives traffic across multiple devices,
acquisition channels, products, and customer segments.

Traffic alone does not guarantee business growth. Users may abandon the
shopping journey between product discovery, cart creation, checkout, and
purchase.

## Objective

Analyze event-level e-commerce behavior to identify the largest conversion
bottlenecks and determine which product opportunities could improve purchase
conversion and revenue.

## Core Questions

1. How effectively do users move through the e-commerce funnel?
2. At which stages do users experience the largest drop-offs?
3. Which devices, channels, products, and user segments underperform?
4. Which opportunities have the highest potential business impact?
5. What product changes should be tested?
6. How should those changes be measured through experimentation?

## Primary Funnel

Session
→ Product View
→ Add to Cart
→ Begin Checkout
→ Purchase

## Expected Outcome

The project will produce:

- Product KPI framework
- Conversion funnel analysis
- Segment diagnostics
- Product performance analysis
- Cohort and retention analysis
- Opportunity sizing
- A/B experiment design
- Statistical experiment analysis
- Power BI executive dashboard
- Product recommendations
